<h1 align="center">Hi 👋, I'm Merlan</h1>
<h3 align="center">Game developer moving into game design for casual F2P games. For 5 years I have built games and interactive products in Unity, and the part I enjoy most is deciding how something should play and then making it feel right. What I’ve made: • Two casual web games on Yandex Games, solo from concept to release: an idle clicker (core loop, upgrade economy, progression curve) and a puzzle built on a single rule that grows through combinations. • Colossal Havoc, winner of Best VR Game at Community Jam: you are a giant attacking a village, with the whole loop built around the fun of physical destruction. • Hit Him, a commercial Steam release with Digital Factory, where I worked on game feel and hit animations and took part in design and balancing discussions. • VR and 2D training simulators for Meta Quest 2, including a dental anesthesia trainer, which turn real procedures into clear step-by-step scenarios. • An interactive Unity 6 digital twin of an air-purification system, which I build as sole developer at the D.V. Sokolsky Institute. Because I’ve implemented and tested my own designs, I know how a feature travels from idea to build, and I can prototype an idea in Unity before anyone else spends time on it. Now I want to grow on the design and product side: metrics, LiveOps and decisions backed by player data. Skills: core loop and mechanics design, progression and economy balancing, tutorial design, rapid prototyping, game deconstruction Tech: Unity, C#, URP, XR Interaction Toolkit / OpenXR, Meta Quest 2, Blender, Git Open to game design roles and internships, remote or in Almaty: merlan01tel@gmail.com</h3>
